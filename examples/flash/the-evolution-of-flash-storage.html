How Flash Writes Data

The quantum mechanics of Fowler-Nordheim tunneling.

1

Electron Injection

Applying high voltage to the control gate to pull electrons onto the floating gate.

2

Floating Gate Trapping

Electrons remain trapped in the isolated gate, maintaining state without power.

3

Threshold Sensing

The presence or absence of charge shifts the threshold voltage, read as 0 or 1.
