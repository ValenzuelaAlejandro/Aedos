Core Mechanics

The Ghost Deck: Leveraging
Negative Effects

The Ghost deck's primary mechanic involves negative effects that, when managed, can fuel powerful synergies.

Ghosts are removed from the deck after use, creating scarcity and forcing strategic choices.

Certain jokers and consumables interact with removed cards, turning a drawback into an advantage.

Focus on building a small, potent deck rather than a large, inconsistent one.

Synergies That Define the Run

The Grim

Mandatory

The Echo

When a Ghost card is played, randomly discard one card and draw a new one.

Recommended

The Reapers

Adds a Ghost card to your discard pile when you play a Spade. Synergistic power.
